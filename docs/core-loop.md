# Core Loop

1. **Observe Turn State**: View 1–5 NPCs, active city theme, hat hand, score multipliers.
2. **Act**: Select hat and flick from bottom to top.
3. **Resolve**: Hit/miss, compatibility, reaction, score gain, city shifts.
4. **Complete Turn**: Show turn summary and consequences.
5. **Choose Buff**: Pick 1 of 3 options to shape next turns.
6. **Advance**: Repeat until Turn 5.
7. **Final Evaluation**: Calculate final score + style grade + build story.

## Loop Quality Targets
- High clarity at every step.
- Fast feedback (<1 sec for basic hit response).
- Strategic legibility (player understands why score changed).
